2. What data I receive

Through the website — almost nothing. The site has no forms, user accounts, analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically log technical data about visits, such as IP address and browser type, to operate and protect the service.

When you contact me and when we study together, I receive:

  • parent or legal guardian data: name, messenger contact, email address or phone number, country and time zone;
  • student data: name, age or grade, skill level, schedule, learning progress and comments on it;
  • payment details: date, amount, payment method and payer’s name. Card numbers and other payment credentials are handled by the payment provider — I do not see or store them.

Lessons are not recorded on video or audio.

3. Why I use the data

  • to reply to your enquiry and run the free trial lesson;
  • to enter into and perform the agreement: scheduling, teaching, and keeping track of prepaid lessons;
  • to send parents progress reports;
  • to comply with legal obligations, primarily tax reporting (issuing receipts in the Russian “Moy Nalog” tax app).

I do not sell personal data, share it for advertising, or send marketing messages without your consent.

4. Legal bases

  • performance of a contract and steps taken at your request before entering into it;
  • consent of the parent or legal guardian — for data of a student who is a minor;
  • compliance with legal obligations — tax records;
  • legitimate interests — website security and protecting my rights in a dispute.

5. Children’s data

Lessons are designed for children and teenagers aged 7–17, but the agreement is made with a parent or legal guardian. I receive a child’s data from the parent or with the parent’s consent. The website does not collect data directly from children.

6. Third-party services

Lessons and communication rely on third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy, and data you share with them is processed under their rules:

  • video calls: Zoom, Google Meet, Yandex Telemost and other platforms as agreed;
  • messengers and email: Telegram, WhatsApp, email;
  • GitHub — students learn to work with Git and GitHub. The student or parent creates the GitHub account themselves, and GitHub processes that account’s data. Under GitHub’s rules, accounts are for users aged 13 and older — younger students work without their own account;
  • the payment provider you use to pay for lessons;
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.

7. International transfers

I work remotely, and the services listed above are located in different countries. Your data may therefore be processed outside your country of residence, including outside the EU and EEA. I only share the data needed for lessons and use major services with their own safeguards.

8. How long I keep data

  • contact and learning data — while lessons continue and for 12 months after the last lesson, then deleted;
  • payment records — for the period required by tax law.

At any time you can:

  • find out what data I hold about you and your child and get a copy;
  • ask me to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask me to delete data unless the law requires me to keep it;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw consent — this does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country, for example an EU supervisory authority or the UK ICO.

To exercise your rights, email me or message me on Telegram. I will reply within 30 days.

California residents. I do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing. The website does not track visitors across other websites, so browser “Do Not Track” signals do not change how data is processed — there is no tracking either way.

10. Security

I keep data only in password-protected accounts, never publish it, and share it with no one except the services listed above. Progress reports go only to the parent named at enrolment.

I may update this policy. The new version is published on this page with its date at the top. I will notify current students in advance of any material changes.
