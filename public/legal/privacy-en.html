11. Cookies and the Meta Pixel

11.1. Cookies and similar technologies, including the browser’s local storage (localStorage), are pieces of data stored on the visitor’s device when the Website is visited and read on subsequent visits.

11.2. The Website uses the following categories of such technologies:

  • strictly necessary — entries in the browser’s local storage that record the visitor’s cookie choice and the selected price display currency. These entries contain no personal data, are not disclosed to third parties and are used without the visitor’s consent, as they are necessary for the operation of the Website;
  • marketing — the Meta Pixel and associated cookies, in particular “_fbp”. The Meta Pixel is provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Ireland) for visitors from the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, and by Meta Platforms, Inc. (USA) for all other visitors (together, “Meta”).

11.3. Marketing technologies are activated solely after the visitor’s express consent, given by clicking “Принять” (“Accept”) in the cookie notice. Before consent is given, and if consent is refused, the Meta Pixel is not loaded and no data is transmitted to Meta. A Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal sent by the visitor’s browser is treated as a refusal of marketing technologies.

11.4. Where consent has been given, the following data is transmitted to Meta through the Meta Pixel: IP address, browser and device information, the URL of the visited page and the referring page, the date and time of the visit, cookie identifiers, and information about events on the Website — page views and clicks on the link to contact the Tutor via Telegram. This data is used to measure the effectiveness of advertising, build advertising audiences and display advertising on Meta services (Facebook, Instagram).

11.5. With respect to the collection of data through the Meta Pixel and its transmission to Meta, the Tutor and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited act as joint controllers within the meaning of Art. 26 GDPR under Meta’s Controller Addendum. Any further processing is carried out by Meta independently in accordance with the Meta Privacy Policy.

11.6. Consent may be withdrawn at any time as easily as it was given, using the “Настройки cookie” (“Cookie settings”) link at the bottom of the Website’s home page. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. The visitor may also delete cookies using browser settings and manage advertising preferences in their Meta account settings.

11.7. Refusing marketing technologies does not restrict access to the Website and does not affect the ability to enter into an agreement for the services.
