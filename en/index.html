FAQ

* Everything parents want to know

What language are the lessons taught in?

Lessons are taught in Russian. They are designed for kids from Russian-speaking families who want to learn to code and keep using their Russian at the same time.

What is the free trial lesson?

The free trial lesson lasts 50 minutes. Your child tries being a beginner developer and builds one of the following: their own web page, a small pie-shop program in Python, or their very first Scratch game.

What if my child doesn’t like it?

That’s exactly why the first lesson is free. If it’s not a good fit, I’ll tell you honestly.

Isn’t it too difficult?

We start from wherever your child is right now.

My child is shy — will that be a problem?

We start calmly, with no pressure. Many kids open up after just a couple of lessons.

Won’t it be too much on top of school?

One or two lessons a week, at a pace that suits your child.

How can I follow my child’s progress?

Once a month, or whenever you ask, I send a progress report through any convenient channel. It covers what your child has learned, what is going well, what needs more attention, and the plan for the next lessons.

Does my child need a powerful computer?

No. Any regular computer or laptop up to 5–7 years old will do, plus a camera and microphone for the video call. All the tools we use run fine even on older machines.

How do the lessons work?

Live 1:1 online lessons over video call, 50 minutes each. Each lesson has three parts: a short theory block, hands-on practice, and feedback with time for questions. This format gets your child to practice quickly and see the results of their own work.

What is the lesson schedule?

The schedule is arranged individually for each student.

What else does my child gain besides coding?

Logical thinking, a structured approach to homework, planning skills and a calmer attitude to mistakes. These skills help across all school subjects — especially English, since programming technologies were originally created in English.
